StudySpace

A Notion-inspired student workspace for grade calculations, assignments, and an academic calendar.

StudySpace ✦
your semester, simplified.

The challenge

Students often manage their academic responsibilities across multiple platforms. Grades, assignments, deadlines, and schedules can become difficult to keep track of when everything is scattered.

StudySpace explores how a single digital workspace could bring these important tools together, helping students stay organized without feeling overwhelmed.

The design approach

The concept takes inspiration from the clean organization and flexibility of Notion while focusing specifically on student needs. The dashboard is organized around course information, grade calculations, upcoming assignments, and an academic calendar.

A limited palette of navy, powder blue, and white creates a calm and consistent visual identity. Clear headings, organized cards, and generous spacing make the interface easier to understand and navigate.

The responsive layout is intended to work across desktop and mobile screens, allowing students to access important academic information on different devices.

Visual direction

Plan. Track. Achieve.

The visual direction focuses on simplicity and organization. Course cards, progress indicators, assignment lists, and calendar sections are proposed interface elements that would help students understand their academic progress at a glance.

These visuals represent an exploratory design concept rather than screenshots of a completed application.

Outcome and reflection

StudySpace is a conceptual student productivity dashboard, not a launched product. The project demonstrates how thoughtful interface design can make academic planning feel more approachable and organized.

Through this concept, I explored how information hierarchy, consistent visual elements, and responsive design can support a more focused student experience.
